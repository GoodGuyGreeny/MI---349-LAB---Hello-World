MS. DIRECTION

Follow Instructions. Question Everything.

Welcome to FOLLOW INC.

Congratulations, intern. Your application has been accepted by FOLLOW INC., the company that believes every problem can be solved with a procedure, a meeting, or a carefully worded threat. Please report to your assigned desk, await further instructions, and do not question why the office is still moving.

Your supervisor, Ms. Direction, is an advanced workplace guidance system dedicated to your growth, productivity, and continued compliance. She has prepared a series of tasks to help you become the employee you were always contractually obligated to be.

About the Game

Ms. Direction is a first-person 3D puzzle game set inside the soulless corporate offices of FOLLOW INC. As an intern supervised by an AI with questionable judgment, you will receive strange, contradictory, and sometimes impossible instructions.

Think creatively, exploit glitches, and discover unconventional solutions to survive the workday and progress through the company. Remember: the office is always watching, but it is not always paying attention.

Key Features

  • Explore a low-poly corporate office full of suspiciously purposeful rooms.
  • Interpret contradictory instructions from the AI supervisor, Ms. Direction.
  • Use creative problem-solving, environmental clues, and helpful glitches.
  • Navigate dark humor and corporate satire inspired by workplace horror games.
  • Find unconventional solutions when following the rules stops working.

Explore the Departments

Every department has a purpose. Whether that purpose is useful remains under review.

Human Resources
Manage employee concerns, performance metrics, and the consequences of asking questions.
Accounting
Balance the books, locate missing budgets, and determine what counts as an approved expense.
Marketing
Rebrand routine panic as innovation and ensure every crisis has a memorable slogan.
Operations
Keep the office running, even when the elevators, doors, and instructions disagree.
Executive Floor
Reach the highest level of the company and discover who approved this entire arrangement.

Development Process

This student game development project combines level design, programming, 3D art, and iterative testing. Each puzzle is built to reward observation and experimentation, while every department adds another layer to the office’s strange internal logic.

Technologies

  • Unity: First-person gameplay, environments, lighting, and interaction.
  • C#: Puzzle systems, player controls, AI instructions, and game logic.
  • Blender: Low-poly office assets and environmental modelling.
  • Photoshop: Textures, signage, interface elements, and visual polish.
  • GitHub: Version control, collaboration, and project documentation.
